Our story

Closed Circuit grew out of more than 30 years of engineering experience in broadcast and telecommunications: TV studios, MCR and PCR control rooms, OB and SNG, satellite and RF. Over time that experience expanded into renewable energy, smart home automation, and the skill of closing high-value deals.

Today we bring it all under one roof: we design, we build, and we stay with our clients until they see results.

Why "Closed Circuit"?

In engineering, current only reaches its goal when the circuit is complete. Business is the same: an idea without design, a design without execution, or execution without follow-up are all open circuits.

Our job is to close the circuit between your problem and the solution, from the first call through commissioning and support.
