What is High-Ticket Closing?

The craft of closing deals for high-value products and services (courses, coaching, consulting, energy systems, real estate...) over phone or Zoom calls.

With people already interested

A closer doesn't chase anyone. They talk to people who asked for information, and help them reach a clear decision.

Online, from anywhere

The work is calls and follow-up on WhatsApp and Zoom. No office, no inventory.

Performance-based

Closers usually earn a commission on each deal they close. Results depend on skill and commitment.

Frequently asked questions

Does the free meeting teach me the skill?

No. The meeting is an introduction: it explains what the skill is and how it works, so you know if it suits you before making any decision.

Do I need sales experience?

No. You need willingness to learn and the ability to communicate. The rest is a skill built through training and practice.

Is there guaranteed income?

No. Closers usually work on commission, and results depend on skill, commitment and the offer they're selling.

How do you work with business owners?

Usually on commission per closed deal, or on a monthly agreement. We decide what fits best on the intro call.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

High-value offers: coaching, courses, consulting, energy systems and B2B services.
