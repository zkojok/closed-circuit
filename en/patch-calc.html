Connector & Patch Calculator

Every port on a patch panel runs Device → Termination panel → Patch panel: 2 cables, 4 connectors and 4 boots per port.

⚡Import from AutoCAD (DXF)

In AutoCAD: Save As → DXF. The file is read in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere. The calculator reads cable numbers written on the lines (e.g. D04109) and termination points (e.g. A/11 or 2/14), then applies Device → Termination (I-BNC) → Patch.
