Frequently asked questions

How fast do you respond?

We aim to reply within 24 hours, and faults that take a broadcast or system down get priority.

Is support free?

Support terms and duration are set in each project's agreement. For systems we didn't deliver, paid support may be available case by case.

Do you support systems you didn't install?

Yes. We can diagnose existing systems and propose fixes after a short intro call.

Is support remote only?

Most issues are solved remotely. On-site visits depend on location and agreement.
