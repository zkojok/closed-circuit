Questions we get every day

Short, honest answers about our services. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

General

Where is Closed Circuit based?

We're based in Beirut, Lebanon, and work remotely with clients across Lebanon, the region and worldwide. On-site work is available in Lebanon, and abroad depending on the project.

How do I get started?

Book a free 20-minute Zoom call at calendly.com/zkojok, or message us on WhatsApp at +961 70 368 646. We'll understand your situation and tell you honestly if we're the right fit.

How fast do you reply?

We reply to every request within 24 hours.

Which languages do you work in?

Arabic and English.

Broadcast & studios

Learn more about this service →

Who is this service for?

TV and radio stations, production companies, universities, religious and government institutions, and creators building a professional studio.

Do you take on small projects?

Yes, from a small podcast or live-streaming studio to a full TV station.

Do you offer consulting without execution?

Yes. You can take the design and quote review only, or have us supervise execution with your supplier.

Can I order the drawings only?

Yes. We produce complete AutoCAD drawings for your project (DWG and PDF) for your team or any contractor to build.

Do you sell equipment without installation?

Yes. You can order devices directly from the ALZTECHPRO store, or buy them with setup, commissioning and training as needed.

Do you work outside Lebanon?

Yes. We work on projects across the region, and the first consultation is available online via Zoom.

High-Ticket Closing

Learn more about this service →

Does the free meeting teach me the skill?

No. The meeting is an introduction: it explains what the skill is and how it works, so you know if it suits you before making any decision.

Do I need sales experience?

No. You need willingness to learn and the ability to communicate. The rest is a skill built through training and practice.

Is there guaranteed income?

No. Closers usually work on commission, and results depend on skill, commitment and the offer they're selling.

How do you work with business owners?

Usually on commission per closed deal, or on a monthly agreement. We decide what fits best on the intro call.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

High-value offers: coaching, courses, consulting, energy systems and B2B services.
